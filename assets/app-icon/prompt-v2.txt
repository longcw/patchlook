Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a finished macOS desktop app icon for Patchlook, a clean native Git diff viewer.
Primary request: redesign the icon for immediate recognition at 16, 32 and 48 pixels. One very bold geometric capital P is the entire foreground. The previous concept had tiny document outlines, code rows, plus/minus badges and multiple colors; discard all those details completely.
Composition: a single centered dark graphite rounded-square app tile, front-facing, occupying approximately 87% of the square canvas with transparent margins. A large warm-white custom geometric P occupies about 63% of the tile width and 72% of its height, optically centered. Very thick strokes, generous square-ish counter with softly rounded corners, unmistakable P silhouette. The stem is a strong vertical bar and the upper bowl is a simple blocky rounded form. Separate the stem and bowl by ONE narrow but clearly visible vertical graphite negative-space seam, hinting at two compared versions, while preserving the unmistakable overall P silhouette. The seam should be wide enough to survive at 32px, about 4% of tile width. Make the counter generous; avoid small holes and thin parts.
Palette: only graphite #252525 and warm white #F2F0E9. No green, no red, no extra accent colors. Strong two-tone contrast. Recognition must come from the large overall silhouette, not tiny detail.
Style: refined, restrained, near-flat, crisp vector-like edges, careful proportions. At most an extremely subtle soft top-edge light on the dark tile, no conspicuous gradients or highlights. No visible bevel on the white mark.
Output: ONE isolated square app icon, ideally 1024 by 1024, with actual alpha transparency outside the rounded-square tile.
Avoid: paper sheets, document outlines, code rows, plus/minus signs, arrows, magnifying glass, eye, Git branch diagram, extra letters or words, annotations, frames around the P, decorative strokes, multicolor effects, gloss, glass, translucent materials, 3D perspective, texture, floor, checkerboard, mockup, grid of alternatives, watermark.
